## What each Monad behavior changes

MIP-8 prices cold storage by 128-slot page. `bidTicks` starts at slot 0 and `askTicks` at slot 256, placing the ladder on exactly four pages. Under MonadNine, reading 512 cold slots costs 512 separate cold reads; under MonadTen, four cold page reads make the remaining ladder reads warm. The measured difference also includes metadata and result writes; the benchmark is an auction, not a bare SLOAD sum. [MIP-8 specification](https://mips.monad.xyz/MIPs/MIP-8), [Monad MIP-8 explanation](https://www.monad.xyz/blog/mip-8).

Gas-limit billing makes a possible self-clear load-bearing for every new order. The measured `placeSelfClear` declared limit is **586,000 gas**. The clients import this bound from `packages/core/src/gas.json`; they cannot choose the smaller no-clear number from the state before inclusion. Gas price remains dynamic. [Monad gas pricing](https://docs.monad.xyz/developer-essentials/gas-pricing). The 7.5% margin comes from [Category Labs' gas-limit analysis](https://www.category.xyz/blogs/setting-your-gas-limit-on-monad); it is guidance, not a protocol guarantee.

Approximately 300 ms blocks make a one-block batch roughly 3.3 auctions per second while there is order flow. A sealed batch still needs a transaction to execute clearing; the keeper supplies one during idle periods. Whole-second RPC timestamps cannot establish an exact subsecond median. [Monad deployment summary](https://docs.monad.xyz/developer-essentials/summary).

Monad uses local mempools rather than one globally propagated pool. This reduces broad pre-inclusion visibility, but does not prove that validators or RPC operators cannot see or react to pending orders. Batchelor's fairness claim instead comes from permutation-invariant allocation after inclusion; no mempool-privacy guarantee is needed. [Monad deployment summary](https://docs.monad.xyz/developer-essentials/summary).

The default client orders transactions by descending total gas price. Priority can influence inclusion or ordering, while all accepted orders in the same batch settle at the same clearing price. A limit and the cutoff pro-rata rule determine fills. This does not promise equal quantities or identical entry blocks. [Monad gas pricing](https://docs.monad.xyz/developer-essentials/gas-pricing).

## Optimization and correctness

The initial MonadTen full-range clear was 718,956 gas. Profiling identified the tagged ladder copy and uncrossing loops as the two largest costs. Caching the batch tag, bounded memory-safe loops, candidate reuse, and exactly two packed result writes reduce it to 342,713 gas. The 250K target remains unmet; this is reported honestly. The same optimized bytecode is compared across both hardforks. Independent reference, permutation, rounding, and invariant tests remain the correctness gate.

The runner reproduced every measurement twice exactly. Full-range ratio: **12.946×**. Order-count variation: MonadNine 0.00%, MonadTen 0.00%.

## Proof page summary

MIP-8 warms storage in pages of 128 slots. Batchelor places both ladders on four pages. The same full-book bytecode clears in 4,436,713 gas under MonadNine and 342,713 under MonadTen. [Monad storage rules](https://docs.monad.xyz/developer-essentials/summary).

Monad charges the declared gas limit. An order therefore reserves 586,000 gas for a possible self-clear, with limits generated from measured fixtures and a 7.5% margin. [Gas billing](https://docs.monad.xyz/developer-essentials/gas-pricing), [Gas-limit guidance](https://www.category.xyz/blogs/setting-your-gas-limit-on-monad).

With approximately 300 ms blocks, one block collects one batch. The first transaction after sealing clears it; a keeper can supply that transaction during idle periods. [Block timing](https://docs.monad.xyz/developer-essentials/summary).

Monad uses local mempools, and priority fees can affect inclusion and ordering. Batchelor proves that reordering accepted orders inside a batch does not change its allocation. Limits and pro-rata cutoffs determine quantities; filled orders share one price. [Mempools and execution](https://docs.monad.xyz/developer-essentials/summary), [Priority ordering](https://docs.monad.xyz/developer-essentials/gas-pricing).
